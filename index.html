Common questions

How do I start a game?

Open the app, tap where you're standing, tap who serves first. That's it — rules (to 11 / 15 / 21, win by 1 or 2) persist from last time and can be changed from the rules chip.

How do I score a rally?

Tap the half of the screen for the team that won the rally. The watch confirms with a haptic and speaks the next call. Mis-tap? Undo restores everything — score, server, positions — from any screen, including game over.

The score is wrong. How do I fix it?

Usually it's a rally or two nobody tapped. Long-press the score to edit it. Tap + or − beside each score, tap the server number to switch between 1 and 2, and tap the player who is serving. Players move to match the score as you go. Tap the check mark to save, or the × to leave the score as it was.

Why does it ask about workouts?

A workout session keeps the app on your wrist for the whole game. When the game ends it is saved to Health as a pickleball workout. That is on by default and can be turned off any time in Settings under “Save workouts to Health.”

How do I stop notifications during a game?

Let your watch do it for you. Every game runs as a workout, so a Fitness Focus set to start with workouts turns on when the game starts and off when it ends. On iPhone, open Settings → Focus, tap +, choose Fitness, then under Set a Schedule tap Workout and turn on “Turn On Automatically.” On the same screen, choose which people and apps, if any, can still reach you. This needs the workout access the app asks for on your first game.

Is it free?

Your first 10 games are free. After that, a single $9.99 purchase unlocks it for good. There is no subscription. Only games where at least one rally is scored count toward the 10.

How do I unlock it?

On your iPhone, open Scoring Buddy and go to the Unlock tab. Apple doesn't allow purchases to be made on the watch itself. Your watch picks up the unlock by itself; if it still asks, open Scoring Buddy on the watch, go to Settings, tap Unlock and then Check Again. On a new phone or watch, use Restore Purchase on the Unlock tab.

I had the app before it cost anything. Do I have to pay?

No. If you installed Scoring Buddy while it was free, it stays free for you, with every feature. If your watch asks you to unlock anyway, make sure it is online, then on the watch open Settings, tap Unlock and then Check Again. Still stuck? Email us and we'll sort it out.

Where is my game history?

In the iPhone app, on the Games tab. Each game is sent from the watch when it ends, with the score, the result and how long it took. You don't need your phone on the court; games catch up the next time the watch and iPhone are near each other. Swipe a game to delete it.

Privacy Policy

Pickleball Scoring Buddy does not collect any data. Effective July 6, 2026. Updated November 23, 2026 for the iPhone app.

  • No data leaves your own devices. The app has no server and no account system. Your game history goes from your watch to your iPhone and nowhere else.
  • The one-time unlock is an Apple in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details, and the app only asks Apple whether the unlock has been bought.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No personal information is asked for or stored — not even player names.
  • Game state, game history and rule preferences are stored only on your devices.
  • If workout saving is on (it can be turned off in Settings), each game is written to Apple Health on your device under Apple's Health privacy protections. The app only writes workouts; it does not read your health data.
  • Since no data is collected, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or delete.

If this policy ever changes, the change will be posted here before it takes effect. Questions: spencelloyd@gmail.com.
