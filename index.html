Common questions

How do I start a game?

Open the app, tap where you're standing, tap who serves first. That's it — rules (to 11 / 15 / 21, win by 1 or 2) persist from last time and can be changed from the rules chip.

How do I score a rally?

Tap the half of the screen for the team that won the rally. The watch confirms with a haptic and speaks the next call. Mis-tap? Undo restores everything — score, server, positions — from any screen, including game over.

Why does it ask about workouts?

A workout session keeps the app on your wrist for the whole game. When the game ends it is saved to Health as a pickleball workout. That is on by default and can be turned off any time in Settings under “Save workouts to Health.”

Privacy Policy

Pickleball Scoring Buddy does not collect any data. Effective July 6, 2026.

  • No data leaves your watch. The app has no server, no account system, and makes no network requests.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No personal information is asked for or stored — not even player names.
  • Game state and rule preferences are stored only on your device.
  • If workout saving is on (it can be turned off in Settings), each game is written to Apple Health on your device under Apple's Health privacy protections. The app only writes workouts; it does not read your health data.
  • Since no data is collected, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or delete.

If this policy ever changes, the change will be posted here before it takes effect. Questions: spencelloyd@gmail.com.
